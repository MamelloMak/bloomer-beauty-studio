Project Overview

Once upon a time, Bloomer was a home-based lash technician until it grew through its clients and social media into a small studio that offers lashes, as well as nail and hair services. The move to Rivonia was made in response to the great demand from the growing population, and all of this was only possible because they offered the best services. As more and more clients were attracted to them, they finally opened another studio in Rivonia, Johannesburg, next to the Sandton studio.

Mission is to offer high-quality professional beauty services while creating a warm and welcoming environment for the guests.

Vision: The dream was to secure its place as the go-to beauty destination, known for its skill, consistency, and care.

Target market: Female South Africans residing in and around Johannesburg, aged between 18 and 40 years old, prioritise convenience and are picky when it comes to service. They fancy a bit of self-care in their lives and, at present, discover and book beauty services mainly through Instagram and word of mouth.

Website Goals and Objectives

Specific goal: Generating a strong online presence to make it easier for new clients to locate Bloomer through organic searches instead of relying on Instagram. Generating leads through service inquiries. Sharing their services and pricing on the website to make clients more knowledgeable before even trying out the services. Helping the growth of the studio's location.

Key performance indicators used to measure success include the number of enquiry form submissions per month, the number of visitors to the Services page (indicating service interest), the homepage bounce rate, and the percentage of enquiries that turn into bookings.

Key Features and Functionality

The index.html file on the website contains the home page's hero image, a short write-up introducing Bloomer, one call to action asking visitors to enquire or view services, and the main navigation links.
about.html has all the information about Bloomers' expansion to Rivonia and the company's vision, goals, and mission.
The services.html page features an extensive list of information about lash, nail and hair services, with each service shown as a card containing an image, description and price.
gallery.html, the gallery page, displays all of the work that was previously done such as hair, nails, and lashes done on previous clients to display the quality of work.
enquiry.html: a form for clients to make an enquiry about a specific service, capturing their name, contact details, service of interest, and preferred date.
Studio contact details on the contact.html page, a contact form, and an embedded map showing the studio location.

CSS OVERVIEW 

Fontd and Colours
1. I used Blush pink `#e8b4b8f5` as the main colour, warm cream `#f7f1ec` as the background and charcoal grey `#3b3b3b` for text and the footer. The palette is from Coolors.
2. For headings i used Playfair Display and for the body, Lato was used, both fonts are from Google Fonts.
3. The colours and fonts are saved in the root in the css page.


Default styles and typography

* A reset with the universal selector sets margin and padding to 0 and `box-sizing` to `border-box`.
* The body uses Lato at 1rem with a line height of 1.6.
* Headings use Playfair Display in bold with a line height of 1.2 and 1px letter spacing. The sizes are h1 2.5rem, h2 1.75rem and h3 1.25rem.
* Paragraphs, navigation links, lists and buttons each have their own font size, weight, line height or letter spacing.

**Layout**

* The header, main and footer have a maximum width of 1200px and are centred.
* The header and navigation use Flexbox, so the logo, name and links sit in a row.
* Each section in main uses Flexbox, so the articles share the space equally.
* The footer uses Grid with three equal columns for contact details, social links and opening hours.

Colour and decoration

* Articles and the aside are white cards with rounded corners and a soft shadow.
* A pink line sits under the header and on the left edge of the aside.
* Links in the main area look like pink buttons, and the forms have borders and rounded corners.
* The footer is dark with light text, and the social icons are turned white so they show up.

**Pseudo classes**

* hover: navigation links change colour and underline, buttons and main links turn charcoal, and footer links turn white.
* focus: form fields and links get a coloured outline.
* active: navigation links and buttons change colour while they are being clicked.

## Responsive Design Overview
