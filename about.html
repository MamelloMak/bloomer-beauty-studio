Our Story

The history of Bloomer is that it started as a home-based business as an eyelash technician. From there, it turned into a studio offering pedicure and manicure services. After that, it introduced professional hair techniques and hair installations. It took some clean work for it to be known as a lash studio and some real hard work for it to be known as a hair and nail studio.

Our Mission

It is our aim to provide premium and professional beauty services in a friendly environment where all our clients can immerse themselves.

Our Vision

To become honorable small-business-class establishment, known for consistency, ability, and genuine care for every client who walks through the doors.
