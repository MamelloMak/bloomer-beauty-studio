Frequently Asked Questions

Select a question to see the answer.

How do I book an appointment?

Fill in the form on our Enquiry page with your details, the service you would like and your preferred date. You will see the price and opening hours straight away, and we reply within 24 hours to confirm your booking.

What are your opening hours?

We are open Monday to Friday from 09:00 to 18:00 and on Saturdays from 09:00 to 14:00. We are closed on Sundays.

Where are your studios?

Our Sandton Studio is at 12 Rivonia Road, Sandton, Johannesburg (011 234 5678). Our Rosebank Studio is at 45 Jan Smuts Avenue, Rosebank, Johannesburg (011 234 5679). You can find both on our Contact page.

How much do your services cost?

Most services cost between R180 and R1,100. Nail art is charged from R50 per nail and braiding starts from R400. The full price list is at the top of this page, and we confirm the final price for any service marked “from” when we reply to your enquiry.

Can I change or cancel my booking?

Yes. Send us a message through our Contact page or call us as early as you can, and we will help you find a new time.
